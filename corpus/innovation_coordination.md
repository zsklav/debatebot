# Innovation and coordination across work arrangements

Gibbs, Mengel, and Siemroth examined idea submissions from over 48,000 employees
at an Indian IT services company. Their event-study analysis compared office,
home-working, and later hybrid periods. During home working, idea quantity
did not change relative to office work, while idea quality declined.

During the hybrid period, submitted idea counts fell. Innovation suffered
particularly in teams with poorly coordinated office attendance. This study
measures submitted ideas rather than general output or retention. It is an
event study in one company, not a randomized comparison of every work setting.
Different outcomes and designs limit direct comparison with hybrid retention trials.
