# Hybrid schedules, retention, and performance

Bloom, Han, and Liang studied 1,612 employees at a Chinese technology company
in a six-month randomized trial during 2021-2022. The hybrid schedule allowed
two home-working days each week. Quitting decreased by one-third and job
satisfaction improved compared with the office group.

The researchers found no performance-grade damage over two subsequent years
and no evidence of different promotion rates. Programmer code output was also
unchanged in their equivalence tests. This concerns a specific hybrid schedule
and workforce, not every occupation or fully remote work. Performance ratings
and code volume are distinct from direct measures of innovation.
