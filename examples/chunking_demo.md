# Remote work: fictional demonstration

This document is an author-written fixture for testing ingestion. It is not
research evidence and must not be used to verify real-world claims.

In our fictional team, working from home removed a daily commute. Some people
used that time for focused work. Others needed more support with coordination.

The fictional observations do not establish that remote work always improves
productivity. A useful study would define productivity, describe its sample,
compare relevant conditions, and report limitations alongside its findings.
